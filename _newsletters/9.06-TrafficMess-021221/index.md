system's false
alarm rate is
so large that
the best way
to deal with
false alarms
is to turn off
the AEB, then
the system
should never
have been
permitted on
public
roadways.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">"...The vehicle operator is
relied on to
intervene and
take action. " Wow!
If Uber's
system
fundamentally
relies on a
human to
intervene,
then Uber is
nowhere near
creating a
Driverless
vehicle.
Without its
own Driverless
vehicle Uber
is past "Peak
valuation".</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">"...The system is not designed to alert the
operator. " That
may be the
only good part
of Uber's
design.  In a
Driverless
vehicle, there
is no one to
warn, so don't
waste your
time.  If it
is important
enough to
warn, then it
is important
enough for the
automated
system to
start
initiating
things to do
something
about it.
Plus, the
Driver may not
know what to
do anyway.
