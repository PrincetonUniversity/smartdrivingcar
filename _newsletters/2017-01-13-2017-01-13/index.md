<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="a-national-hybrid-activityagent-based-demand-model-to-characterize-the-mobility-of-the-united-states"><a href="http://orfe.princeton.edu/%7Ealaink/Theses/SeniorTheses_IndWork%2717/final_independent_work_marocchini.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">A National Hybrid Activity/Agent-Based Demand Model to Characterize the Mobility of the United States</a></h3>
<p>K. Marocchini'18, Jan. 11, "...The goal of the model
presented is to generate the precise origin, destination,
and arrival/departure time for every trip made by every
individual on a typical workday when school is in session.
More generally, the model provides the spatial and
temporal distribution for every citizen in the United
States. Every individual simulation produces a unique trip
file that contains an individualized, probabilistic record
of every person-trip on an average weekday, which is
expected to total to just over 1 billion trips. Each
record includes every trip the person makes including
spatial coordinates of the origins and destinations as
well as the exact departure and arrival times in seconds
after midnight. Files also contain pointers that indicate
individual characteristics about a given person, including
income, household location, familial status, etc...." <a href="http://orfe.princeton.edu/%7Ealaink/Theses/SeniorTheses_IndWork%2717/final_independent_work_marocchini.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a> </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmm...
Excellent!! The ~1B trips are at <a href="http://orf467.princeton.edu/NationWideTrips%2716/" rel="noopener noreferrer">NationWideTrips'16</a>
.  Those <a href="http://orf467.princeton.edu/NationWideTrips%2716/NationWidePersonTrips_oPixel_oTime_SubCounty/" rel="noopener noreferrer">200 miles &amp; under</a>and <a href="http://orf467.princeton.edu/NationWideTrips%2716/LongTripPixelData/" rel="noopener noreferrer">over 200 miles</a> are sorted by &#123;oPixel, oTime&#125; such
that all personTrips originating from a pixel in a
county are together in a single file. (Of course, not
so for trip destinations. No file has greater than ~1M
trips/rows so each is openable in excel they are easy
to look at.)  Characteristics of the <a href="http://orf467.princeton.edu/NationWideTrips%2716/PersonDataModule1/" rel="noopener noreferrer">308M travelers are available in homeCounty files</a>.  <a href="http://www.princeton.edu/%7Ealaink/Orf467F16/FinalProject/Orf467F16FinalProjectAnnouncement.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">See below, Upcoming Events</a>, for analyses made
possible by Kyle's model. Florida DoT should give
Kyle an internship this summer  :-)  Alain</p>
<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="training-self-driving-cars-on-the-streets-of-los-santos-with-gta-v-just-got-easier"><a href="https://techcrunch.com/2017/01/11/training-self-driving-cars-on-the-streets-of-los-santos-with-gta-v-just-got-easier/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Training self-driving cars on the streets of Los Santos with GTA V just got easier</a></h3>
<p>J. Mannes, Jan 11, "...One of the biggest advantages of
training off of a virtual environment is that its primed
for harvesting labeled data. Objects in GTA V, whether
traffic signs or cyclists, can easily be bounded and
analyzed... One Princeton student, <a href="http://orfe.princeton.edu/%7Ealaink/TRB%2717/Using_GTAV_to_Learn_Distances_TRB_Final.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">Artur Filipowicz</a>'17,
specifically made use of this to collect 1.4 million
images of stop signs in a variety of conditions to predict
the chance a given intersection will have a sign present
and to estimate the distance to the given sign.... <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2017/01/11/training-self-driving-cars-on-the-streets-of-los-santos-with-gta-v-just-got-easier/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a> </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmm... The real
value of using a virtual environment to attach
affordances (labels) to image data (pixel arrays) is
that you know with certainty and accuracy what and
where everything is relative to the image plane in the
virtual environment.  It is all your creation.  Plus,
it is substantially/infinitely easier to create
'corner cases' in a virtual environment and perform
any number of variational/sensitivity  analyses than
to try to recreate them in reality.  Chenyi Chen*16
deserves the credit for pioneering this approach in
his <a href="http://orfe.princeton.edu/%7Ealaink/Theses/chenyiPhDfinal_ExtractingCognitionOutOfImagesForThePurposeOfAutonomousDriving.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">PhD dissertation</a> and <a href="http://deepdriving.cs.princeton.edu/paper.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">seminal paper</a>.  Artur Filipowicz'17 began his wok with
GTA5 last year as his Junior Independent Work.  Alain</p>

<p>W. Knight, Jan 12, "The computers that control

information about the physical properties of roads by
studying Google Street View and comparing the scenes to

intersection should be based on images captured by
Google's mapping cars.</p>
