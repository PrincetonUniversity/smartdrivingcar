<p>I wanted to make sure you saw the exciting news
 out from Waymo (formerly Google self-driving cars) today. We just announced that we're launching the first public trial of our self-driving cars. Starting today, anyone in the Phoenix area can apply to be part of

<p>Over the last eight years, Waymo has been focused

<a href="https://medium.com/waymo/accelerating-the-pace-of-learning-36f6bc2ee1d5" rel="noopener noreferrer">improving the performance of our software</a>. Now, with Waymo's early rider program, we're turning our focus to riders.
 Everything we learn in this program will help bring us closer to launching a truly self-driving car into the world, and realize the potential of this technology to make it safer and easier for everyone to get around.</p>
