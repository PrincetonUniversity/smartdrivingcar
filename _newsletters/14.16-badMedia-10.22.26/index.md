[HandyRides](https://handyrides.com/welcome) Update

Alain Kornhauser, Sept 26, "... …" Hmmmm… .In
 our 3rd month serving Spelman College in Atlanta.  All going extremely well.  I've traveled by car more than 18k miles so far this year.  93% (17k miles) has been driven by "assisted driving".  I've intervened for about a dozen perceived safety concerns.
 Half were misperceptions and didn't need my interventions and half I abandoned my initial intervention either realizing that I had mis-perceived and was about to create a safety challenge or the automated emergency capabilities precluded me from creating a
 safety problem.  The two most egregious have been: #1.  when I  forced it to make a right turn on red while failing to look back to my right when two young laddies, walking 2 dogs, had appeared "out of nowhere in the crosswalk".  If the "assisted driving"
 had not kicked in, my head may not have turned in time for me to see them.  It saved my butt!! (and theirs!!!!).  #2.  I was in the center of a 3-laned, one-way road, stopped at a head of a red light. Cars were stopped at the light on my right.  Left lane
 was free with giant fire truck coming up fast with sirens blasting. Light turns green.  Car doesn't move, so I start nudging it forward, but it won't go even though the fire truck s]was rapidly passing me on my left. But, "Assisted driving" precluded me from
 nudging forward because it, not me, realized that the firetruck was turning right from the far left lane and cutting me off.  Had it not kept me from nudging the car forward, I would have caused a major crash.  I was clueless that it was going to make that
 right turn, but it saw it, acted to keep me from moving forwward and saved my butt once again.  I am totally convinced that it drives better than I drive.  It wins as far as I'm concerned.  It has earned the victory and Elizabeth agrees. 🙂
 Elizabeth & Alain.
