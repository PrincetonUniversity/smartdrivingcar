<p>A. Ciceu,
April 28, "The
<a href="https://planetprinceton.com/2021/04/08/nj-transits-princeton-transitway-study-is-underway/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Princeton Dinky</a>, the

<p>The study's

to examine
potential
alternatives
for the

<p>According to
NJ Transit,

alternative
transportation
currently
under

roadway with
embedded rail
that can

and

rail corridor

parallel
roadway for

tram and/or
bus service"
and "a roadway

guideway that
could support
a rubber-tired
tram and
buses."</p>
<p>Another
option, also
outlined in
the NJ Transit
study's plans,

the Dinky

fixture of
Princeton
transit: "A
no-build

continues to
use the
existing Arrow
III cars or
similar rail
vehicle."</p>
<p>However, this

the
decision-making

transit

Dinky....</p>
<p>"Any future
service that
.. is more

walk ...
whether future
transit
vehicles have
rubber tires,
steel wheels,
magnetic
cushions, or

else,"
Bottigheimer
