<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="the-impact-of-vehicle-automation-on-carbon-emissions"><a href="https://www.americanprogress.org/issues/green/reports/2016/11/18/292588/the-impact-of-vehicle-automation-on-carbon-emissions-where-uncertainty-lies/?ReillyBrennanFoT" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Impact of Vehicle Automation on Carbon Emissions</a></h3>
<p>M. A-Kearns, Nov 18, "...We found that existing research does not draw clear and consistent conclusions about the impact of autonomous vehicles on the environment generally and climate specifically...." <a href="https://www.americanprogress.org/issues/green/reports/2016/11/18/292588/the-impact-of-vehicle-automation-on-carbon-emissions-where-uncertainty-lies/?ReillyBrennanFoT" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a> </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmm... While this is a very thorough recap of the literature, it falls into the trap of not clearly differentiating the fundamental different forms of automation. It is bad enough that the SAE, and former NHTSA, 'Levels' are totally confusing. Casually and interchangeably using self-driving, autonomous, automated, etc. makes it unreadable. This report would be much improved if they had taken the literature and carefully categorized it into 3 distinct types:</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Safe-Driving Cars (trucks &amp; buses too) that only have automated collision avoidance and automated lane keeping that are purely focused on not allowing the driver to crash the vehicle.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Self-Driving ... focused on allowing the driver to relinquish the driving tasks in well defined circumstances/driving scenarios, and</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Driverless... which enables shared-ride mobility as a service.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Placed in these categories, it is clear that Safe-Driving technology will have negligible effect on the environment, Self-driving will enable a substantial increase VMT (because the personal disutility of travel will be substantially reduced while having no opportunity to promote ride-sharing. So VMT must increase and continue to increase as the Self-Driving systems improve. Driverless enables centralized real-time management of fleets of autonomous Taxis that will offer mobility as a service at very attractive prices (because there is no driver to pay.) While person miles traveled will substantially increase because of the affordability of the mobility and its availability to all, that increase may be more than offset by the ride-sharing that is uniquely available with this technology. Alain</p>
