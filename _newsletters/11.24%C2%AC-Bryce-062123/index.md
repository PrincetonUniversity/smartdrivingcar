<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="article-1"><a href="https://twitter.com/BrandonTidd/status/1670445258638004224" rel="noopener noreferrer">A Driverless Contest for Mid-Size Cities</a></h3>
<p>K. Pyle, June 14, "A benefit of travel is the random conversations with strangers that cause one to look at the world in a slightly different way. For instance, standing in the airport security line this week, a lady from Little Rock, AR explained that Uber and Lyft no longer serve the hometown of the Bill Clinton Presidential Library.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.clintonairport.com/parking-transportation/ground-transportation/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fact check, according to its website, Lyft and Uber</a>, as well as other local providers serve the Clinton National Airport.</p>
<p>She said their apps indicated that their respective services were not available. She believes this happened as a result of the pandemic.</p>
<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="article-2"><a href="https://www.thv11.com/article/money/uber-lyft-drivers-little-rock-isnt-profitable-place-drive/91-b242a74c-19c8-4e70-82b4-0f3466be7a5f" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fact check; Perhaps there still is a dearth of drivers as reported in 2021.</a></h3>
<p>She described the taxi service in the Little Rock area as "awful". It takes an hour and a half to get one. She also doesn't feel safe in a taxi especially compared to Uber/Lyft…</p>
<p>If her perception of the limited mobility choices is representative of the population, perhaps Little Rock would be a great use case for a driverless service. I forwarded this question to Princeton Professor Kornhauser and Michele Lee of Cruise for them to ponder and look forward to any feedback they might have (Kornhauser comments about this in the latest <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XP7ti2tO4S4&amp;t=1238s" rel="noopener noreferrer">SmartDrivingCars</a> podcast).</p>
<p>As background, the three of us serendipitously converged at <a href="https://viodi.com/2023/03/24/designing-mobility-for-the-next-billion-passengers/" rel="noopener noreferrer">CES2023 and talked about mobility challenges</a>. In a soundbite from that interview, Lee explains the challenges and opportunities for improving mobility and questions whether she could make the journey to Alain's house. There are glimpses of her entering and securing her wheelchair in the Cruise, driverless Origin vehicle…." <a href="https://twitter.com/BrandonTidd/status/1670445258638004224" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a> </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmmm… Check out <a href="https://youtu.be/MQ8kukG_5rM" rel="noopener noreferrer">ZoomCast322</a> below. Be sure to also look at Ken's embedded video with Michelle. Alain</p>

<ul>
<li>
<p>SafeDrivingCars: Those in which the automated function are explicitly designed to not only substantially reduce driver misbehavior by constraining the performance characteristic such as incorporating <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=speed+governor&amp;rlz=1C1GCEB_enUS791US791&amp;oq=speed+governor&amp;aqs=chrome..69i57j0i512l9.4112j0j7&amp;sourceid=chrome&amp;ie=UTF-8" rel="noopener noreferrer">speed governors</a> that only permit excessive speeds in geofenced locations such as <a href="https://www.theglen.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Watkins Glen International</a> and stretches of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autobahn" rel="noopener noreferrer">German Autobahn</a>, but also automatically intervene to prevent crashes; thus, extending what is done today with <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-lock_braking_system" rel="noopener noreferrer">anti-lock brakes</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_stability_control" rel="noopener noreferrer">electronic stability control</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>SelfDrivingCars: Those which allow the driver for some extended period of time to be "feet-off" the brake &amp; throttle, delivering to the driver substantial comfort &amp; convenience, but also "hands-off" the wheel for shorter periods of time providing a little more comfort &amp; convenience. Absolutely required are "eyes &amp; brain" focused the human task of driving, ready to intervene should the automated driver begin to fail.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>DriverlessCars: Those which serve only passengers and/or goods from trip start, through finish. Luckily, the notion that individuals might own such vehicles for personal use and/or be able to "AirB&amp;B" them for others to get from A to B is now realized by essentially everyone as exceedingly naïve. The <a href="https://group.mercedes-benz.com/innovation/product-innovation/technology/article-2.html" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mercedes booth at this year's CES</a> showed no sign of its <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eftN0lwNk88" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mercedes Benz F 015 Luxury in Motion | LIVE PREMIERE CES 2015</a>. Yea!</p>
</li>
</ul>
