<p>J. Kanet,
Nov 9,
"Advanced
Driver
Assistance
Systems (ADAS)
have improved
automobile
safety by
minimizing the
factor most
frequently
associated
with car
accidents –
human error.
The
conventional
wisdom has
been that ADAS
technologies
should
mitigate the
number of
insurance
claims, but
the impact of
ADAS on claim
severity has
been less
clear. ADAS
features
include
expensive and
complex
technical
components and
are often
installed on
exposed areas
of vehicles,
making them
susceptible to
damage from a
collision.</p>
<p>This white
paper examines
the
multivariate
effect of ADAS
features on
claim
severity. It
is a follow up
to our earlier
report, <a href="https://lexisnexis.turtl.co/story/multivariate-adas-whitepaper/?utm_campaign=INSATO20.MetStraDem.VBDemGenWP" rel="noopener noreferrer">ADAS Analysis Creates Path for Auto Insurance Rating</a>,
which analyzed
the impact of
ADAS systems
on claim
frequency. For
both analyses,
LexisNexis
Risk Solutions
considered the
same sample of
11 million
vehicles from
model years
2014-2019, and
reviewed
industry-wide
claims loss
data for
bodily injury,
property
damage and
collision
coverages.
For this
follow-up
report, we
looked at the
interactions
between a core
list of ADAS
features and
quantified the
claim severity
differential
across all 648
possible
combinations
of those
features. ...
" <a href="https://risk.lexisnexis.com/insights-resources/white-paper/true-impact-of-adas-features-on-insurance-claim-severity-revealed?trmid=INSATO21.2021AutoLG.2021ADAS.OMXX-545501" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a>  </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmmm... Excellent.  Finally there is
data that
supports that
ADAS is indeed
fundamentally
good for the
insurance
industry in
that it allows
them to make
more money.
Not to be
crass, but
"crash
mitigation"
(airbag, <a href="https://www-nrd.nhtsa.dot.gov/pdf/esv/esv17/Proceed/00137.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer">energy absorbing car design</a>, ...) has been
great for
safety...
saving lives,
reducing
occupant
severity , ...
but,
the
liabilities
associated
with crash
mitigation
have
increased.  <a href="https://www.iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-auto-insurance" rel="noopener noreferrer">Claim Severity</a>
for both Physical
Damage and
Bodily Injury
have gone
up.   Cars are
more expensive
to repair and it
costs more if
you live than
if you die.
🙁,.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">For years I've been arguing that ADAS focused
squarely on
Crash
Prevention &#123;automated
collision avoidance
(that actually
works well...
does kick
automatically
to avoid
collision 😎),
not just
"warning"
(that is
annoying.
Because since it is just a warning
there is no
pressure to
make it work
well... just
cover your
butt!  So, no
pressure to
make it work
well. Consequently,
"everyone"
turns it off
to avoid being
annoyed... 🙁&#125;
is
Automation's
best
opportunity to
deliver real
safety...
Save lives,
...,  and make
$$$ for the
Insurance
industry!</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">For years, I've postulated that as soon as &#123;"the
expected

&lt; (less

"incremental

(difference

my insurance
agent (the <a href="https://www.geico.com/auto-insurance/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gecko</a>, <a href="https://bestlifeonline.com/flo-progressive-real-life-news/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Flo</a>,
<a href="https://www.njm.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">NJM</a>,
or ???) should
insist on
picking up the

price

continue to

existing
premiums!
Nothing but a
win-win!  I
win because
the
probability
that I die...
is lower and
the <a href="https://www.geico.com/auto-insurance/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gecko</a>,
<a href="https://bestlifeonline.com/flo-progressive-real-life-news/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Flo</a>,

of the object
is uncertain,
then all bets
are off.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">I

Tesla's
perception
algorithm
disregards all
stationary
objects ahead
when leading
as well as
when

NHTSA has to
tell Tesla to
not do that
any more!!!
Tesla must go
back,
essentially to
the beginning,
and figure out
how to
reliably
determine if
it can pass
under, beside
or over
stationary
objects
detected in
the road
ahead.  Alain</p>
