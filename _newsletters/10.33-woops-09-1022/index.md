<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="self-driving-tech-company-floats-possible-sale-to-apple-or-microsoft">Self-Driving Tech Company Floats Possible Sale to Apple or Microsoft</h3>
<p>D. Welch, Aug. 30, "Aurora Innovation Inc.'s chief executive officer recently laid out a range of options for the self-driving company to respond to worsening market conditions and partners pushing out timelines, including a possible sale to Apple Inc. or Microsoft Corp., according to a document seen by Bloomberg.</p>
<p>Chris Urmson, who co-founded Aurora after running Google's self-driving car project, also outlined cost cuts and floated measures including taking the company private, spinning off or selling assets and pursuing a small capital raise in a memo labeled "board discussion pre-read" and dated Aug. 3. Urmson inadvertently sent this to staff and asked them on Aug. 9 not to open it, the document shows.  ..." Read more </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmmm... Realistic but not good news. With revenue at zero all of these companies are struggling. The annual addressable market of new class 8 trucks in the US is about 275,000 units. Given the large number of competitors addressing a limited market that has yet to yield any revenue for any of them suggests that this is a really tough business, especially if first revenue remains a year or more in the future.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">What may be even more daunting is competition from a <a href="https://www.inverse.com/innovation/tesla-semi-price-specs" rel="noopener noreferrer">TeslaSemi</a> with "FSD.Class8", not to mention the <a href="https://waymo.com/waymo-via/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Waymo Via</a> initiative. They both can cross subsidize their driverless Class 8 initiatives with their driverless people movement investment.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">I still contend that there is a substantial near-term revenue opportunity Advanced Professional Driver Assistance focused on improving Professional Driver workplace. <a href="https://www.osha.gov/trucking-industry" rel="noopener noreferrer">OSHA</a> should be mandating such technology. Aurora could be generating revenue from it today. CEOs of trucking companies could be paying for it today and pocketing extremely attractive RoIs. Professional Drivers would be happier campers. So much so that the driver shortage might disappear. Alain</p>

<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="why-self-driving-cars-have-stalled">Why self-driving cars have stalled</h3>
<p>The Guardian, Sept. 8, "Fully fledged self-driving technology appears to be perpetually just around the corner. It is a promise that the Tesla chief executive, Elon Musk, has made almost every year since 2013....</p>
<p>But in the real world, it is still an open question whether level five self-driving automation is actually possible. Josh Toussaint-Strauss finds out how close industry frontrunners have come to full automation and assess the scale of the problems standing in their way...." Read more </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmmm... A good video highlighting the challenges of trying to do Self-driving all the time, everywhere.  I claim that it is silly to try to do anything all the time, everywhere. Consequently, an easy video to produce.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Self-driving "all the time, everywhere" hasn't stalled.  Its always been and will always be a pipe dream.  (I've never been able to drive my own car "all the time, everywhere" even though Madison Avenue has forever suggested that I can  (or should be able to if I wasn't such a ... ))</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">Self-driving doesn't need to be "all the time and everywhere" because there is a driver in the car.  Let them do the driving at times and places where the system doesn't work very well.  In fact, more should be done to not allow them to drive either in heavy snow, torrential downpours, dense fog, when they are tired, when they've had too much to drink, smoke or snort.</p>
<p class="newsletter-commentary">We need to get to a point at which Driverless (Self-driving in an Operational Design Domain (ODD)) actually delivers safe origin to destination mobility in some ODD that delivers real value to society. Let's define the ODDs where this technology does work.  Put it to work there and begin recouping some value from all of the sunk investment.  Then focus on the most efficient and effective way to grow the ODDs such that they most expeditiously generate the most value to society. Alain</p>
