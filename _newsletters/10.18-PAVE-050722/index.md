<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="pave-virtual-panel-avs-and-public-good-trenton-moves"><a href="https://pavecampaign.org/event/avs-and-public-good-trenton-moves-2/" rel="noopener noreferrer">PAVE VIRTUAL PANEL "AVS AND PUBLIC GOOD: TRENTON MOVES"</a></h3>
<p>PAVE, May 4, "Autonomous vehicle technologies offer incredible potential: they could make our highways safer, they could offer new mobility options for people who can't drive, and they could help create a more equitable transportation system for those who are not well-served by our current system.</p>
<p>During the month of May, we are highlighting places where AVs are in use — today — being deployed, tested, and used for public good. We want to look at examples of the technology being used to serve food deserts, to expand access to rural communities, to offer new accessibility options, and more.</p>
<p>We are starting with the Trenton MOVES initiative, which is the first large-scale urban transit system in America based entirely on self-driving shuttles. The shuttles, which carry four to eight passengers, serve traditionally underserved Trenton neighborhoods, where 70% of households have limited access to a single automobile, or no access at all. Our panelists will detail the program, describing how it works, the results it has achieved, and their vision for the future......" <a href="https://pavecampaign.org/event/avs-and-public-good-trenton-moves-2/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read more</a> </p><p class="newsletter-commentary">Hmmmm...
