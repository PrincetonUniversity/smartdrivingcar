Getting from A to B in a car today requires the person behind the wheel perform two (2) tasks

* Place the steering wheel at the correct angle and depress the brake or the throttle the right amount.  This must be done every 20 milliseconds or so during the entire journey with the primary
 objective being "don't crash" and numerous secondary objectives such as get to B at a reasonable time, don't run out of gas, try not to break too many traffic laws, be nice to your fellow road users, ….  Let's call this the "20 millisecond" task, and
* Make some command decisions, such as traffic is really bad, should I turn around and go back home", or the road is closed ahead, should I go through the gap in the barricade since I am inside
 the closure or my ultimate destination is short of the blockage, or no, I'd better turn around and find another way to B, It's raining really hard and the road ahead is likely to flood so I'd better stop or go another way, or there's a hose across the road
 and let me figure out the better of two bad options that I face, or, I'm involved in a fender bender, should I leave the car where it is or ask it to pull over to the side, or… Let's call this the "Supervisor" task.

We have reached a point that some of our "Level 2+" systems, such as (Supervised) FSD perform the
millisecond task while still challenged to perform the supervisor task as well as some of us would like.

It also turns out some/many people can perform the
supervisor task as well as ever but are really challenged in performing the
millisecond task as well as they once did and really shouldn't be driving because they are a substantive safety problem.

Society currently deals with these situations by revoking the driving privilege that these folks have enjoyed "since they
 were 16 years old".   That is NOT anything any society likes doing, and society has been paying a heavy safety price balancing that "rock & hard place"

We in New Jersey have begun to work on looks to be a much more elegant solution… a staged operators licenses … reversing
 the process use for going from not driving to driving by passing through a "learners" stage.  In this case, creating an intermediate "supervisor" while transitioning from driver to the final revoking of all operating privileges.

The supervisor privilege would be granted to those who can perform very well the "supervisory" tasks of operating a vehicle
 that can perform the "20 millisecond" task. This is not only "life saving" for those who really should not be driving: senior, folks suffering for the early forms of Parkinson's, infrequent seizures, many if not most, of the blind, me ((Supervised) FSD performs
