And L.A. is next</a></h3>
<p>R. Mitchell, June 26, "Robotaxis keep tangling with firefighters on the streets of San Francisco, and the fire chief is fed up.</p>
<p>"They're not ready for prime time," Chief Jeanine Nicholson said….</p>
<p>State regulators track <a href="https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/autonomous-vehicles/autonomous-vehicle-collision-reports/" rel="noopener noreferrer">robotaxi collisions</a>, but they don't track data on traffic flow issues, such as street blockages or interference with firetrucks.</p>
<p>But the Fire Department does. Since Jan. 1, the Fire Department has logged at least 39 robotaxi incident reports…</p>
<p>State regulators track <a href="https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/autonomous-vehicles/autonomous-vehicle-collision-reports/" rel="noopener noreferrer">robotaxi collisions</a>, but they don't track data on traffic flow issues, such as street blockages or interference with firetrucks.</p>
<p>The Fire Department incidents include reports of robotaxis:…</p>
<p>Safety data censored</p>
<p>In 2021, the DMV joined with Waymo on a <a href="https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2023-06-01/driverless-trucks-california-dmv-mistrust-safety-regulations" rel="noopener noreferrer">court-approved deal</a> to allow driverless car companies to censor not only trade secrets but basic information on safety performance, including most details of collision reports as well as information on how the company handles driverless car emergencies.</p>
<p>The industry is tight with the information it releases to the public about its operations on public roads.</p>
<p>Waymo won't say how many cars it runs in San Francisco. Cruise said it operates 150 to 300 cars but won't be more precise. Neither company will say how large its fleet will grow, or how quickly. Neither Waymo nor Motional will say how many robotaxis they're testing in Santa Monica and L.A…."<br/>

<p>SafeDrivingCars: Those in which the automated function are explicitly designed to not only substantially reduce driver misbehavior by constraining the performance characteristic such as incorporating <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=speed+governor&amp;rlz=1C1GCEB_enUS791US791&amp;oq=speed+governor&amp;aqs=chrome..69i57j0i512l9.4112j0j7&amp;sourceid=chrome&amp;ie=UTF-8" rel="noopener noreferrer">speed governors</a> that only permit excessive speeds in geofenced locations such as <a href="https://www.theglen.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Watkins Glen International</a> and stretches of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autobahn" rel="noopener noreferrer">German Autobahn</a>, but also automatically intervene to prevent crashes; thus, extending what is done today with <a href="https://r.search.yahoo.com/_ylt=AwrCwohlmLlj7mwA9B8PxQt.;_ylu=Y29sbwNiZjEEcG9zAzEEdnRpZAMEc2VjA3Ny/RV=2/RE=1673136358/RO=10/RU=https%3a%2f%2fen.wikipedia.org%2fwiki%2fAnti-lock_braking_system/RK=2/RS=jmEmjts4v9aKd5qA3i1zu6hL7_I-" rel="noopener noreferrer">anti-lock brakes</a> and <a href="https://r.search.yahoo.com/_ylt=AwrCwDOMmLljUWAAnAcPxQt.;_ylu=Y29sbwNiZjEEcG9zAzEEdnRpZAMEc2VjA3Ny/RV=2/RE=1673136396/RO=10/RU=https%3a%2f%2fen.wikipedia.org%2fwiki%2fElectronic_stability_control/RK=2/RS=mBTcrDpZh4zs51KHC5IvDOlsIpU-" rel="noopener noreferrer">electronic stability control</a>.</p>

Absolutely required are "eyes &amp; brain" focused the human task of driving, ready to intervene should the automated driver begin to fail.</p>
