<h3 class="newsletter-article-title" id="thursday">Thursday,</h3>
<p>July 16 @ 2pm New York Time</p>
<p>SAFE
invites you to join us for the release of "<a href="https://us02web.zoom.us/webinar/register/5915943028608/WN_bUHzsV9BSm-tJkKQ6QVNqg" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fostering Economic Opportunity through Autonomous Vehicle Technology</a>", our new paper that
addresses how autonomous vehicles (AVs) can redress
imbalances for low-income communities, whose options are
limited by inadequacies in today's transportation.</p>
<p>To
mark the launch of the paper, SAFE is holding a <a href="https://us02web.zoom.us/webinar/register/5915943028608/WN_bUHzsV9BSm-tJkKQ6QVNqg" rel="noopener noreferrer">panel event</a> on July 16 at
2:00pm EDT.</p>
<p>Alisyn
Malek, SAFE, will be moderating a conversation between:</p>
<p>Courtney Ehrlichman, CEO, Ehrlichman Group</p>
<p>Dr. Richard Ezike, Senior Policy Associate, The Urban
Institute</p>
<p>Dr. Alain Kornhauser, Director of the Program
in Transportation, Princeton University</p>
<p>SAFE
would appreciate you joining us for what we expect will be
an illuminating discussing on transportation's role in
limiting economic opportunities for millions of Americans,
and how AVs and autonomous transit can be part of the
solution.</p>
