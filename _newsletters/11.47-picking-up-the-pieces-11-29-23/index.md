<p>M. Sena, Nov. 26, "IT HAS BEEN a rough couple of months for CRUISE LLC after it reached a high point in August this year. CRUISE's license was for 300 vehicles. Then the wheels began to come off the cart, figuratively speaking. On Sunday evening, the 19th of November, CRUISE founder and CEO, Kyle Vogt, announced that he was throwing in the towel. Is this the beginning of the end for CRUISE? Or is this just the end of the beginning, as most of the Pollyannaish opinion pieces on the subject have opined?</p>
