<p>Brussels, 12 February 2014 "...Two European standards organizations, ETSI and <a href="http://www.itsstandards.eu/" rel="noopener noreferrer">CEN</a>, confirmed today that the basic set of standards requested by the European Commission to make connected cars a reality has been fully completed.
